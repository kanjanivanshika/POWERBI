## 🛫SkyTrack – Airline Operations & Flight Delay Intelligence ##

## Project Objective ##
The objective of this project is to analyze airline flight data and provide meaningful insights into flight operations, airline performance, departure and arrival delays, cancellations, airport efficiency, route performance, delay reasons, and operational trends.

The Power BI report will help airline management understand:
- Total number of flights operated.
- Airline-wise flight performance.
- Departure and arrival delay patterns.
- On-time performance across airlines.
- Flight cancellations and diversions.
- Airport-wise operational efficiency.
- Route-wise flight performance.
- Delay reasons and their impact.
- Monthly, weekly, and daily flight trends.
- Peak hours and high-delay periods.
- Average flight duration and distance.
- Operational performance across different airlines and airports.
  
The report will help airline management identify operational inefficiencies, understand flight disruption patterns, and make data-driven decisions to improve scheduling and operational performance.
